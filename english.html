About me

I graduated as a dentist from the Academic Centre for Dentistry Amsterdam (ACTA) in 2023. My approach to dentistry is based on scientific knowledge, modern techniques, and personal attention. For me, good dental care is not only about maintaining a healthy mouth, but also about giving patients the confidence to smile freely again.

When you are in my chair, it is important to me that you feel heard and at ease. I take the time to listen to your wishes and concerns so that together we can find the treatment that best suits your needs.

I work in North Holland and provide dental care for patients of all ages. In addition, I work as a self-employed dentist for dental practices and oral healthcare organizations, including Tandzorg Lief, Poetz Jeugdmondzorg, and De Mondzorg Poli. In these different settings, I am committed to delivering high-quality patient care and maintaining a pleasant collaboration with dental teams.

Dental treatments

At Tandzorg Tamimi you can receive various forms of general dental care.

Periodic oral examination

Regular check-ups to assess your oral health and identify dental problems at an early stage.

Preventive oral care

Guidance and advice focused on maintaining a healthy mouth.

Restorative dental care

Restoration of damaged or affected teeth with attention to function and long-term durability.

Endodontic dental care

Treatment of root canal problems with the aim of preserving natural teeth.

Paediatric dental care

Personal guidance and treatment for young patients.

Aesthetic dentistry

Treatments focused on improving the appearance of the teeth, tailored to your personal wishes.

Acute dental complaints

Assessment and treatment of acute dental complaints.

Treatment of anxious patients

A calm and personal approach for patients who experience anxiety around dental treatments.

Privacy and Cookie Statement

1. Introduction

Tandzorg Tamimi considers the protection of your personal data important. We handle the information you share with us carefully and confidentially.

This privacy and cookie statement explains which personal data we process, why we use this information and which rights you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Which personal data do we process?

When you contact Tandzorg Tamimi or receive dental care, we may process personal data.

  • Name and address details
  • Telephone number
  • Email address
  • Date of birth when necessary for providing care
  • Information about your oral health and medical background
  • Information you provide during contact or appointments

Health information is only processed when necessary for providing dental care.

3. Why do we use your data?

  • Answering questions and requests
  • Planning and providing dental treatments
  • Maintaining a patient record
  • Communicating about appointments and treatments
  • Carrying out necessary administrative activities

4. Contact form

This website contains a contact form. The information you provide is used to respond to your message.

For processing contact form submissions, we use Formspree. Your information is processed according to the privacy policy of this service.

5. Retention of data

We do not retain personal data longer than necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

For medical records, the legal retention period according to Dutch healthcare legislation (WGBO) applies.

6. Sharing personal data

Tandzorg Tamimi only shares personal data when necessary for treatment, when you have given permission or when legally required.

7. Security

We take appropriate measures to protect your personal data against loss, misuse or unauthorized access.

8. Cookies

The Tandzorg Tamimi website currently does not use cookies for advertising or marketing purposes.

If additional services such as analytics or marketing tools are used in the future, this privacy and cookie statement will be updated.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, correct or request deletion of your personal data when legally possible.

For questions regarding your personal data, please contact:

tandzorgtamimi@hotmail.com

10. Complaints

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your personal data, please contact us first. You also have the right to submit a complaint to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
