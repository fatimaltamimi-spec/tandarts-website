Dental treatments

At Tandzorg Tamimi you can receive a wide range of general dental treatments, including:

Periodic oral examination

Regular check-ups to assess your oral health and detect problems at an early stage.

Preventive oral care

Guidance and advice focused on maintaining a healthy mouth and preventing dental problems.

Restorative dental care

Restoring damaged teeth with attention to function, durability and aesthetics.

Endodontic dental care

Root canal treatments aimed at preserving teeth and relieving dental complaints.

Paediatric dental care

Personal guidance and treatment for children with attention to a positive dental experience.

Aesthetic dentistry

Treatments focused on improving the appearance of your smile according to your personal wishes.

Acute dental complaints

Assessment and treatment of urgent dental problems to relieve pain and prevent further issues.

Treatment of anxious patients

A calm and personal approach for patients who experience anxiety during dental treatment.

Privacy and Cookie Statement

1. Introduction

Tandzorg Tamimi considers the protection of your personal data important. We handle the information you provide with care and confidentiality.

This privacy and cookie statement explains which personal data we process, why we use this information, and what rights you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Which personal data do we process?

When you contact Tandzorg Tamimi or receive dental care, we may process personal data.

  • Name and address details
  • Telephone number
  • Email address
  • Date of birth when necessary for providing dental care
  • Information regarding your oral health and medical history
  • Information you provide when contacting us or scheduling appointments

Health-related information is only processed when necessary for providing dental care.

3. Why do we use your data?

Your personal data may be used for the following purposes:

  • Answering questions and requests
  • Planning and providing dental treatments
  • Maintaining a patient record
  • Communicating about appointments and treatments
  • Carrying out necessary administrative activities

4. Contact form

This website contains a contact form. The information you provide is used only to respond to your message or request.

For processing contact form submissions, we use Formspree. Your information is processed according to the privacy policy of this service provider.

5. Retention of personal data

We do not store personal data longer than necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

For medical records, we follow the legal retention period according to Dutch healthcare legislation (WGBO).

6. Sharing personal data

Tandzorg Tamimi only shares personal data when this is necessary for providing care, when you have given permission, or when required by law.

7. Data security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against loss, misuse or unauthorized access.

8. Cookies

The Tandzorg Tamimi website currently does not use cookies for advertising or marketing purposes.

If additional services such as analytics or marketing tools are used in the future, this privacy and cookie statement will be updated accordingly.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to request access to your personal data, correct inaccurate information, or request deletion when legally possible.

For questions or requests regarding your personal data, please contact us:

tandzorgtamimi@hotmail.com

10. Complaints

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your personal data, please contact us first so we can address your concerns.

You also have the right to submit a complaint to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
