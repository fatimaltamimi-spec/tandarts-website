Dental treatments

At Tandzorg Tamimi you can receive a wide range of general dental treatments, including:

Periodic oral examination

Regular check-ups to assess your oral health and detect problems at an early stage.

Preventive oral care

Guidance and advice focused on maintaining a healthy mouth and preventing dental problems.

Restorative dental care

Restoring damaged teeth with attention to function, durability and aesthetics.

Endodontic dental care

Root canal treatments aimed at preserving teeth and relieving dental complaints.

Paediatric dental care

Personal guidance and treatment for children with attention to a positive dental experience.

Aesthetic dentistry

Treatments focused on improving the appearance of your smile according to your personal wishes.

Acute dental complaints

Assessment and treatment of urgent dental problems to relieve pain and prevent further issues.

Treatment of anxious patients

A calm and personal approach for patients who experience anxiety during dental treatment.
