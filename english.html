About me

I am Fatima Altamimi, graduated as a dentist from the Academic Centre for Dentistry Amsterdam (ACTA) in 2023.

I have developed a broad interest in general dentistry and believe in combining scientific knowledge, modern techniques and personal attention.

I provide dental care for patients of all ages and I am available as a self-employed dentist for dental practices in Noord-Holland.

Dental treatments

At Tandzorg Tamimi you can receive a wide range of general dental treatments, including:

Periodic dental examinations

Regular check-ups to assess your oral health and detect problems at an early stage.

Preventive dental care

Guidance and advice focused on maintaining a healthy mouth and preventing dental problems.

Restorative dentistry

Restoring damaged or affected teeth with attention to function, durability and aesthetics.

Endodontic care

Root canal treatments aimed at preserving teeth and relieving dental complaints.

Paediatric dental care

Personal guidance and treatment for young patients with attention to a positive dental experience.

Aesthetic dentistry

Treatments focused on improving the appearance of your smile according to your personal wishes.

Acute dental pain complaints

Assessment and treatment of urgent dental problems to relieve pain and prevent further issues.

Treatment of anxious patients

A calm and personal approach for patients who experience anxiety or tension during dental treatment.
